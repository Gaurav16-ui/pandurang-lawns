About Pandurang Lawns

Celebrate Your Special Moments With Us

Welcome to Pandurang Lawns, where your dream ceremonies unfold in scenic grandeur. Conveniently situated, our picturesque venue offers expansive manicured lawns, majestic ambient illumination, dedicated culinary dining courtyards, and lavish staging designed to make every auspicious milestone memorable.

Whether preparing for an intimate ring ceremony or a multi-day grand royal wedding, our venue offers versatile configurations, seamless logistics, and warm hospitality to delight your guests from entry to farewell.
